AI Made Simple

Your Guide to Every AI Tool — by Faraz Ali Khan

So many AIs. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, Sora... and a new one every week. Everybody is talking about them. But almost nobody tells you the one thing you actually need to know: which AI should YOU use, and for what?

If that question has ever left you confused, this book was written for you.

AI Made Simple is a plain-English guide that finally makes sense of the AI world. No jargon. No technical theory. No computer science degree required. If you can send a message on your phone, you can use every single tool in this book.

What makes this book different

Most AI books explain how AI works. This one shows you what to DO with it. Think of AI tools as a workshop full of expert workers. One is a brilliant writer. One is a talented artist. One makes videos, one composes music, one writes code, one does your research. The secret is knowing which expert to call for which job. That is exactly what you will master here.

What you'll find inside

  • ✓ One expert per chapter — the best AI for writing, images, videos, music, coding, studying, presentations, and office work
  • ✓ One FREE option and one PAID option for every task, so you know when free is enough
  • ✓ Ready-made prompts in every chapter — copy, paste, change a few words, done in five minutes
  • ✓ Simple diagrams that explain each idea at a single glance
  • ✓ Special chapters for students, job seekers, business owners, and content creators
  • ✓ An honest chapter on AI's biggest mistakes, and exactly when NOT to trust it

Who this book is for

Students who want to study smarter and finish projects faster. Professionals who want to save hours every week. Shop and business owners who want to grow without hiring a team. Content creators who want to post consistently. Parents, job seekers, and complete beginners who are tired of feeling left behind by technology. If any of that sounds like you, you are holding the right book.

What you'll be able to do

By the last page, you will not just know the names of AI tools — you will actually be able to use them. Write a professional email in seconds. Design a poster without any design skills. Turn your chapter into an audio podcast. Build a small website by just describing it. Make a birthday song with someone's name in it. Practice for a job interview at 2 AM. And most importantly, you will know when to trust AI, and when to double-check it.

AI is not the future anymore. It is the present. The only real question is whether you will use it, or watch others use it and wonder how they got ahead.

One book. Every AI tool. Simple words. Turn the page, and take the lead.
